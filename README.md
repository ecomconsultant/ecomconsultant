# Hi, I'm Jon Pastorizo 👋

**Operations & Ecommerce Consultant | Process Improvement | Practical AI & Automation**

I help business owners improve operations, simplify workflows, and make better use of their existing people, systems, and technology.

My background spans mechanical engineering, manufacturing process improvement, team and operations leadership, and ecommerce management. I use a diagnosis-first approach: understand the workflow, identify root causes, prioritize meaningful changes, and automate only where it genuinely helps.

## What I work on

- **Business operations:** process mapping, operational diagnostics, handoffs, roles, and practical planning
- **Process improvement:** root-cause analysis, DMAIC, waste reduction, and repeatable workflows
- **Ecommerce operations:** Shopify, multichannel operations, fulfillment, integrations, and troubleshooting
- **AI & automation assessment:** choosing appropriate tools and automation opportunities based on business value and risk

## How I think about improvement

**Diagnose → Analyze → Prioritize → Improve**

More technology is not always the answer. First make the work understandable, measurable, and manageable. Then consider what should be eliminated, simplified, standardized, assisted, or automated.
